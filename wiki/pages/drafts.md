---
title: Drafts
type: note
tags: [format, workflow]
---
Writing that is not finished goes in `inbox/`, which sits inside the knowledge
base (KB) root and outside `pages/`. A draft is not a page: it is not indexed,
not searched, not exported, and not checked for broken links or missing fields.

That leniency is the point. A half-written file has broken links by definition,
and a tool that complained about them would be complaining about the ordinary
state of working.

A draft becomes a page when it is promoted into `pages/`, and at that moment
every rule applies at once. See [[Structure]].
