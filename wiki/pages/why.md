---
title: Why Stemma
type: principle
aliases:
  - the case for stemma
tags: [design]
---
Notes kept for years are read by someone who was not there when they were
written, and usually that someone is their author. A note that says what is not
enough; what is missing is why, and any note worth keeping carries a reason.

The bet this tool makes is that a body of notes is worth maintaining in its own
right — contiguous, citation-bearing, and plain enough on disk that no program
has to exist for it to still be readable. That is the argument Bush made for the
memex [@bush1945], minus the machine he could not build.

Two things follow from it, and both are refusals:

- The tool never writes prose. It resolves, checks, rewrites links and reports,
  and the words stay yours.
- The tool never destroys what it does not understand. An unfamiliar field, a
  comment, a file it has no business reading: all of it survives untouched, and
  that is the single most important promise in the format. See [[The format]].
